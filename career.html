🎯 Core Responsibilities

🛡️
  • Real-Time Traffic Analysis: Conduct continuous surveillance on enterprise event logs, network packets, and firewall activities to flag indicators of compromise (IoCs).
  • Incident Triage & Investigation: Classify alert severity levels systematically, isolate suspicious network endpoints, and perform root-cause correlation diagnostics.
  • Vulnerability Mitigation Management: Spearhead scheduled architecture patch deployments, scan for system logical flaws, and document infrastructure exposures.

  • Threat Documentation & Compliance: Compose structured post-incident reporting protocols to ensure organizational network blueprints align with active cybersecurity governance.
