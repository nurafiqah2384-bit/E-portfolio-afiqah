Why AirAsia?

I aspire to work as a SOC Analyst at AirAsia because the company operates a large-scale digital ecosystem that includes online booking platforms, mobile applications, customer databases, payment systems, and aviation-related technologies. As a Network Security student, I am particularly interested in monitoring networks, detecting cyber threats, investigating security incidents, and protecting organizational assets. The SOC Analyst role aligns closely with my interests and technical background, as it involves continuous monitoring, threat detection, incident response, and security analysis.
